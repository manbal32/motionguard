Commercial use, modification and redistribution are permitted. [Official pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- The bundled GLB is the texture-free repack of `Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf` documented by the upstream avatar project. SHA-256 `d6f3b64cab629f6ddca9ba063811aefd3663a8a4e49ade47cd9505dc2bbd70f`. Swing3D assigns its own colors at runtime. [Repack provenance](https://github.com/programasweights/avatar/blob/main/ASSETS.md)
- Fairway tiles and course geometry are generated by Swing3D. They have no external asset dependencies.

Vendored so the viewer runs offline and behind restricted networks; no CDN is used. [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js)
